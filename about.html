About        RISD E’Ship is a student-led organization dedicated to cultivating entrepreneurial spirit within the RISD community. Through programs like Speaker Series, NYC Immersion, Founder's Sandbox, and Founder's Chat, we connect students with industry leaders, alumni, and each other — creating space to learn how creative careers and studios are built, explore early opportunities, and start conversations that carry beyond campus.
